PROVEN PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS (Targeting AMD/Xilinx Artix-7):

- Silicon Footprint: 6 LUTs, 4 Registers (0.03% device occupancy).
- Latency Overhead: 1 to 3 clock cycles (10ns to 30ns at 100MHz).
- System Integrity: Built-in 3-cycle stability filter against glitches.
